Warranty Policy

At **enext**, we stand behind the quality of our products. All enext devices come with a limited manufacturer's warranty.

Warranty Period

Our standard warranty period is **12 months** from the date of original purchase. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

What is Covered

  • Hardware components failures.
  • Manufacturing defects.
  • Battery defects (dropping below 80% capacity within the first year).

What is NOT Covered

  • Accidental damage (e.g., cracked screens, liquid damage).
  • Unauthorized repairs or modifications.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Loss or theft.

How to Claim

To initiate a warranty claim, please contact our support team at **support@enext.tech** with your order number and a description of the issue.
